This is a real OpenStreetMap basemap (via MapLibre GL JS + OpenFreeMap, no API key required), recolored to match the Scriptorium palette. Roads, coastline, lakes, and turloughs are real survey data, not illustrated. Major road names are shown; minor roads, tracks, and route-number shields are hidden to keep the map quiet. Nearby markers merge into a numbered cluster at low zoom — click one to zoom in and split it apart. The "Routes" menu in the top bar suggests themed trails between sites — open a site's drawer and look for "Part of…" to jump to a trail it belongs to. Several coordinates are approximate pending field verification (dashed ring): Seven Streams of Teeskagh, Carran Turlough, the Carron and New Quay village centres, and St Brigid's Well. Eyebright and "Burren Karst" describe a species and a landscape, not a single point, so they aren't pinned.
Select a marker to see its field notes.
